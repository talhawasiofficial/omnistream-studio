Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 27, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to OmniStream Studio ("we", "our", or "the Service"). OmniStream Studio provides content creators and media producers with tools to manage, schedule, and publish video content across supported digital platforms, including TikTok.

2. Information We Collect

When you use OmniStream Studio to connect with TikTok, we request access through official OAuth 2.0 authorization protocols:

  • Basic Profile Information (user.info.basic): Used solely to identify your connected creator account and display your profile handle and avatar in the studio dashboard.
  • Content Publishing (video.publish & video.upload): Used exclusively when you choose to publish or draft a video file from OmniStream Studio to your TikTok account.

3. How We Use Information

We use authorized data strictly to provide the requested content scheduling and publishing services. We do not sell, rent, or monetize creator data, nor do we use creator content for advertising purposes.

4. Data Storage & Security

All authentication tokens are transmitted via secure HTTPS and stored with industry-standard encryption. Access tokens are kept strictly confidential and used only to perform operations explicitly initiated by the user.

5. User Control & Data Deletion

Creators may revoke OmniStream Studio's access at any time through their TikTok account privacy settings or by contacting our support team. Upon revocation or deletion request, stored tokens are permanently removed.

6. Third-Party Services

OmniStream Studio integrates with the TikTok API. Your use of TikTok features is governed by the TikTok Terms of Service and TikTok Privacy Policy.
